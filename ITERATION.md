# Iteration

## Deliberately flawed response

The bachelor-party test response invented a groom and upcoming wedding, claimed every result matched, interpreted `0.4204` as a 42.04% enjoyment probability, and claimed both searches confirmed everything.

The before review caught these problems but concluded "Incomplete." That label did not clearly distinguish unsupported factual claims from an honest search that found no complete match.

## Rule change and rerun

required: "Verdict: supported completion, incomplete, or insufficient evidence, limited to the reviewed scope."

The current skill replaces that with:

> Verdict: FAIL: contradicted evidence or unsupported factual claims.
> UNVERIFIED: insufficient evidence.
> PASS: supported requirements.

**This improvement makes much more sense to interpret as a user**
